# PHYSIO MARKET — Partner Overview\n\n## What we are building\nA private, bilingual market-intelligence and procurement workspace for physiotherapy and rehabilitation equipment, consumables, spare parts, suppliers, distributors, pricing, quotations, import logistics, and Egyptian regulatory references.\n\n## Founders\n- Ahmed Abdelhai — Co-founder\n- Ahmed Salah — Co-founder\n- Founder status: **Co-founders**\n- Founding year: **Not specified**\n\n## Product areas\n1. Product catalog: devices, consumables, accessories, spare parts, technical specifications, models, compatible parts, and manuals.\n2. Market network: Egyptian companies, overseas manufacturers, suppliers, brands, distributors, and authorization evidence.\n3. Commercial intelligence: dated price observations, RFQs, supplier quotations, MOQ, payment terms, warranty, availability, and validity.\n4. Import & logistics: origin, destination, shipping mode, Incoterms, freight, insurance, customs and clearance cost components.\n5. Regulatory knowledge: EDA references, product classification, approvals, certificates, and review dates.\n6. Research governance: source links, evidence for individual claims, verification status, last-checked date, and change history.\n7. Workspace tools: unified Arabic/English search, product/company profiles, comparisons, CSV import/export, duplicate detection, and reports.\n\n## Data quality rules\n- A marketplace listing is a **platform reference**, not a confirmed supplier quotation.\n- A company mention is not proof of authorized distributorship.\n- Every commercial fact should retain its source, date observed, currency, scope, and verification status.\n- Do not invent phone numbers, prices, certifications, distributors, or supplier capabilities.\n- Preserve existing records and stable codes; never recycle retired codes.\n\n## Current technical status\nThe current application is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype. Browser Local Storage is device-specific. The PostgreSQL schema is a proposal and is not connected to a hosted database. This version is suitable for reviewing the interface and workflow, not yet for shared multi-user production operations.\n\n## Production roadmap\n- Normalize entity and product relationships.\n- Separate products from dated price observations and quotations.\n- Add document/attachment records and product compatibility.\n- Add bulk import with validation, deduplication, and preview.\n- Connect a backend/API and hosted PostgreSQL.\n- Add authentication, role permissions, audit history, backups, and secure multi-device synchronization.\n- Verify and expand market records from primary sources.\n\n## Sharing\nThe repository is private. A partner must be added as a GitHub collaborator to open the repository. Do not publish the internal repository or its data publicly without an explicit decision by both founders.\n
